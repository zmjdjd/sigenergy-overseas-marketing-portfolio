# Sigenergy Overseas Marketing Portfolio

## Project Overview

This project is a portfolio case study focused on the overseas marketing strategy of a Chinese energy technology brand entering the European market.

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate practical skills in:

- Market research
- Competitor analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Social media marketing
- Content strategy
- KOL / influencer outreach
- English marketing communication
- Marketing performance analysis

## Target Market

Europe, with a primary focus on Germany and other major residential energy storage markets.

## Project Modules

1. European Energy Storage Market Research
2. Competitor Analysis
3. Target Customer Analysis
4. Social Media Strategy
5. 30-Day Content Calendar
6. KOL / Influencer Research
7. Outreach Email Samples
8. Marketing KPI Analysis

## Goal

To simulate a real overseas marketing project for a Chinese renewable energy technology company and build a practical portfolio for international marketing roles.
